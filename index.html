    How to use

    What you need

    • A microphone
    • Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Firefox, Brave and Opera won't work.

    Getting started

    1. Wear the Voice to Chat prim (right-click it → Wear or Add).
    2. A pop-up and a chat message give you a link. Click it. If it opens inside the Second Life viewer, copy the link the page shows into Chrome or Edge.
    3. The first time, the page asks Use this prim? and shows your name. Click Use this prim.
    4. Press Turn On Mic and allow the microphone.
    5. Talk! Each sentence appears in chat when you pause.

    Keep this page open while you play. It can sit behind the viewer, but don't close it.

    After a teleport

    In a new region the prim gives you a new link. Click it, and your open page switches over by itself. You can close the extra tab.

    Clean chat

    Hide bad words is on unless you switch it off under Settings. Swear words and rude, grown-up, violent or drug words are removed, or swapped for clean ones like heck, darn or jerk so your sentences still read well.

    The mic sometimes mishears "six" as "s*x", so in English it's always posted as six.

    Troubleshooting

    Nothing shows in chat
    Touch the prim → Get Link and click the link again. Also check it's turned on.
    "Microphone blocked"
    Click the icon at the left of the browser's address bar, allow the microphone, then press Turn On Mic again.
    My chat name looks different from my display name
    Second Life only allows plain letters, numbers and simple symbols in object names, and the prim's name is what labels your chat. Fancy letters, symbols, emoji and other alphabets would show as ?, so they're left out. If nothing readable is left, your username is used instead. To choose another name, touch the prim → Set Name and type it with plain letters. Your words themselves can still use any characters.
    It stops working on some land
    Scripts are switched off on no-script land.

    Keep it safe
    • Never share your link. Anyone who has it can post in chat as you. If you share it by accident, touch the prim → New Link.
    • Only click Use this prim for links from your own prim.

    Talking tips

    • Pause briefly between sentences. Each sentence is posted when you pause.
    • Under Settings, pick the Language that matches your accent, like English (US), (UK), (Canada) or (India).
    • English isn't your first language? Still pick an English option, and try a few to see which one understands you best.
    • Does the mic keep getting the same word wrong? Add it under My word fixes.
    • Very fast or mumbled words can be misheard. Short words at the start of a sentence get missed most.
    • Wear headphones, so the mic doesn't pick up Second Life music or voice chat.
    • If it mishears you a lot, try the other browser: Chrome and Edge use different speech services.
