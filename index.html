Voice to SL

Talk into your microphone and your words appear in Second Life local chat.

  1. Wear your Voice to Chat prim in Second Life.
  2. Click the link it gives you in chat. This page opens with your prim's address filled in.
  3. Press Start listening and talk. Each sentence is posted when you pause.
