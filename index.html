How to use

What you need

  • A microphone
  • Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Firefox, Brave and Opera won't work.

Getting started

  1. Wear the Voice to Chat prim (right-click it → Wear, or Attach to HUD).
  2. A pop-up and a chat message give you a link. Click it. If it opens inside the Second Life viewer, copy the link the page shows into Chrome or Edge.
  3. The first time, the page asks Use this prim? and shows your name. Click Use this prim.
  4. Press Turn On Mic and allow the microphone.
  5. Talk! Each sentence appears in chat when you pause.

Keep this page open while you play. It can sit behind the viewer, but don't close it.

After a teleport

In a new region the prim gives you a new link. Click it, and your open page switches over by itself. You can close the extra tab.

Troubleshooting

Nothing shows in chat
Touch the prim → Get Link and click the link again. Also check it's turned on.
"Microphone blocked"
Click the icon at the left of the browser's address bar, allow the microphone, then press Turn On Mic again.
Your name shows as ?????
Touch the prim → Set Name and type a name using plain letters.
It stops working on some land
Scripts are switched off on no-script land.
Keep it safe
  • Never share your link. Anyone who has it can post in chat as you. If you share it by accident, touch the prim → New Link.
  • Only click Use this prim for links from your own prim.

Your browser's own speech service turns speech into text (Google in Chrome, Microsoft in Edge). The text then goes straight from your browser to your prim. This website never receives it.
